The occasion

MSP opened its doors in 2011. Fifteen years and one unmistakable round logo later, we're getting the family back together for an afternoon at Tapijn: alumni of every year and staff old and new.

The timing is no accident. Maastricht University celebrates its 50th birthday this year, so consider this MSP's contribution to the party season.

The practical bits

  • Where: Tapijn, Tapijnkazerne 20, 6211 ME Maastricht. Open in Google Maps
  • When: Saturday 10 October 2026, 15:00 to 19:30. Programme starts 15:30.
  • Cost: €5, and that includes snacks and your first two drinks. You pay after registering (we send the link); extra tokens at the bar, cards welcome.
  • Getting there: About twenty-five minutes on foot from Maastricht station, the last stretch through the city park; ten from the city centre. Limited parking nearby; the bike still wins. Full directions here.
  • Dress code: None. Fifteen years of MSP has taught us not to try.
